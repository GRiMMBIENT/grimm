FM-1 / DEATHDREAM / V2.0

Free firmware that turns the M-VAVE FM-1 into a machine for dark ambient, dungeon synth and deathdream.

INSTALL WEB EMULATOR EDITOR

INSTALL IN A MINUTE

  1. Back up first. Open the editor, connect the FM-1 and press BACKUP.

  2. Open the FM-1 Firmware Hub in Chrome or Edge and connect your FM-1 over USB.

  3. Pick GR!MM and press install. Your sounds and samples stay when you update.

Unofficial firmware, not made or supported by M-VAVE. Install at your own risk. You can always go back to the stock firmware through the Firmware Hub.

THE COMBOS TO KNOW

HOLD FX + BLACK KEY
A track plays by itself (GENERATE), always in key.
HOLD FX + WHITE KEYS
Freeze, reverb and echo throws, tape stop, reverse, filter sweeps, stutter.
HOLD SEL
One key plays a whole chord.
SEL + OCT+
LATCH on or off, from any page.
HOLD SAVE + WHITE KEYS
Recall or save project slots A-D (while playing, a recall waits for the next bar).
HOLD GLO + WHITE KEYS
Mute or solo T1-T4.

NEW IN V2.0

  • GENERATE: tracks that play by themselves, drifting, pulsing or in a storm.
  • Real choirs: Gregorian monks, nuns and a cathedral, sung by real singers.
  • GLIDE on every sound, and a second line, ring and noise for PHASE.
  • A master filter, four kinds of distortion and a new mixer with tape and drive per track.
  • MIDI clock in and out, MIDI over the TRS jack, velocity.
  • Tape that slips like an old VHS, cleaner fades, and candles that burn with the sound.
